# Competitions

We are proud members of [United Club Gymnastics (UCG)](https://unitedclubgymnastics.org/), a national organization dedicated to supporting adult and collegiate club gymnastics across the United States. UCG provides opportunities for gymnasts of all ages, experience levels, and abilities to continue training, connect with the gymnastics community, and compete. Competition is encouraged but is not required to be part of our club!

UCG offers competition in four disciplines: Women's Artistic Gymnastics (WAG), Men's Artistic Gymnastics (MAG), Trampoline & Tumbling (T&T), and Unified Artistic Gymnastics (UAG). UCG also offers a Decathlon competition format for gymnasts interested in competing across all ten artistic gymnastics events. For more information about competition levels, skill requirements, and scoring, visit the [UCG Competition Rules](https://unitedclubgymnastics.org/rules/).

You'll often find us competing at the local William & Mary Invitational in Williamsburg, UCG National Championships, and other events hosted by UCG-affiliated clubs and colleges throughout Virginia and the Mid-Atlantic region. Check the [UCG Season Calendar](https://unitedclubgymnastics.org/events/) for upcoming sanctioned competitions and community events throughout the season.
